# Reading the D1 registers

The [grahas](planets/catalog.yaml), [signs](signs/catalog.yaml), [houses](houses/catalog.yaml), [nakshatras](nakshatras/catalog.yaml) and [padas](padas/namakshar.yaml) supply chart vocabulary and lookup data. A planet in a house, the lord of that house, and its natural significator are distinct roles. For example, the Moon can occupy the fourth house while another graha rules its sign. A graha's occupation alone establishes neither dignity nor strength.

## From positions to evidence

Use the convention recorded with the engine result. For the default D1 profile, use sidereal Lahiri positions and whole-sign houses. Normalize each unrounded sidereal longitude into [0°, 360°). For a longitude in arcseconds, index the sign by floor(longitude / 108000), the nakshatra by floor(longitude / 48000), and the pada within it by 1 + floor(longitude / 12000) mod 4. The sign and nakshatra catalogue numbers are one-based. Use full precision before division. Each lower boundary belongs to the new cell. At 30° the sign changes to Taurus while Krittika continues into its second pada.

For one-based sign numbers A (ascendant) and P (graha), the occupied house is 1 + ((P − A) mod 12). For house H its sign is 1 + ((A + H − 2) mod 12). Its lord is the ruler in the sign table. The twelve [lordship entries](lordships/) explain the first house for every ascendant. For a house from the Moon, substitute its sign for A and attach a Moon-origin fact key.

For a [full aspect](aspects/full-graha.yaml) count inclusively from the aspecting planet's sign to the target sign. Saturn in Pisces can aspect Taurus by its third aspect. Reversing source and target tests a different relation. The [dignity table](dignity/sign-dignities.yaml) distinguishes sign membership, exact exaltation point and own-sign rulership. Retrograde motion is a separate longitudinal-speed fact. For combustion, use the [natal thresholds](states/combustion-orbs.yaml) and [motion and station rules](states/combustion.yaml) of the selected profile.

## Birth-time precision

| Input | Eligible evidence |
|---|---|
| Exact local birth time | Resolve the historical time zone and ambiguous clock times first. Then compute the natal positions, ascendant, houses and their dependents. |
| Approximate, with a supported interval | Check every relevant transition throughout the interval. Emit a fact only if its value is stable. An ascendant crossing can invalidate a house lord while leaving the lunar nakshatra usable. Keep the precision flag in the explanation. |
| Approximate, without a known interval | Ask for the possible range or apply the unknown-time policy. A ±5 minute varga sensitivity check cannot supply a missing birth-time interval. |
| Unknown | Use local noon only as the PRD calculation anchor. Suppress ascendant, natal houses, house lords and dependent matches. For other discrete facts evaluate the full local civil day in the birth-place zone, including DST. Suppress the naming syllable if the lunar pada changes, even when the nakshatra is stable. |

A stable relation may survive a change in one of its inputs if the engine can establish the relation across the entire interval. Equal values at two endpoints alone are insufficient. A missing fact produces unknown and the public reading can explain the limitation.
